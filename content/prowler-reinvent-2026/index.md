### Who I want to meet

- **Prowler** team, users, and contributors — what's working, what's missing, what should ship next
- **Security and cloud** practitioners (AWS, Azure, GCP, M365, K8s, IaC, SaaS) — posture, prioritization, compliance without the spreadsheet hell
- **Community** folks from the open-source and AWS circles — especially if we've only ever chatted online
- **Potential customers** — if you're weighing a **read-only posture review**, multi-cloud onboarding, or how open checks map to your frameworks (CIS, SOC 2, ISO, NIS2, …)

### What I bring

Hands-on work as a **full-stack / AWS** builder and **Prowler partner**: production experience with **Prowler Cloud** and the open engine (**2,500+ checks**, **34 providers** — see [Prowler Hub](https://hub.prowler.com)). I like concrete problems: scope, connect read-only, interpret findings, fix order — not fear slides.
